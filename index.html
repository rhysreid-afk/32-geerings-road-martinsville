The Story

This Is What Space
Actually Feels Like

Most acreage asks you to choose:
cleared land to use, or forest to disappear into.
32 Geerings Road, gives you both.

The property sits on 13.17 hectares (32.54 acres) across two titles, Nestled on the edge of Watagan National Park amongst temperate rainforest, with freshwater creek frontage and approx. 6 acres of cleared land surrounding the home, swimming pool and sheds, opening out to views of the forest, the mountain range and passing wildlife.

Freshly renovated, the home has a huge entertaining area with large decking on 3 sides, so wherever you're standing, you're looking out into nature and filling your lungs with beautiful fresh air.

Inside, the main living area opens beneath high ceiling, with walls of glass that slide onto the deck. A quality slow combustion fireplace warms the whole living area. It's the kind of room that erases the line between inside and out: morning coffee, afternoon light, evening quiet, all framed by trees instead of fences.

The master suite is its own retreat. A walk-in robe with room to spare, and a striking ensuite with a rain shower + and window looking out over the forest.

Down the hallways, the original bathroom keeps its character: a deep red clawfoot bath, timber wainscoting, brass fittings, and a window that frames nothing but grass and gum trees.

Outside, the property earns its acreage: a 40,000 litre in-ground saltwater pool fully fenced with its own pool shed, a remote-controlled extra-large double garage with workshop, a large machinery shed, and a wood-splitting shed complete with a 40-ton wood splitter and winch. Five + rainwater tanks hold approx. 100,000 litres, and an approx. 160 sqm organic vegetable garden sits fully enclosed under wire, so there's room for all of it, the boat, the caravan, the tractor, the dirt bikes, the toys.

This is the type of home and property that inspires you to feel peaceful, calm and connected to nature, yet is only moments' drive to schools and all amenities. It's 1.5 hours' drive to Sydney Airport, and going north, just 45 minutes to Newcastle and the domestic and international airport.
